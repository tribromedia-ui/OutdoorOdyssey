About Us

Outdoor Odyssey is built by three brothers who grew up in the woods, on the water, and under open skies. From rugged campsites and winding Missouri trails to river runs and quiet mornings in the deer stand, the outdoors has always been our home.

We created Outdoor Odyssey to share that world — the adventures, the gear, the challenges, the laughs, and the moments that make the wild worth exploring. Whether we’re hiking, kayaking, camping, or reviewing the tools that help us get there, our mission is simple: inspire others to step outside and chase their own odyssey.

Thanks for joining us on the journey. The trail is open — let’s walk it together.
